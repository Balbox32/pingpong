How it works

Three layers and three drops.

A plant-based top, a wool felt pad and a steel clip. The oil goes on the felt, which releases it slowly through the day.

  1. 1

    Drip

    Add three to five drops of your blend to the felt. Let it soak in for a minute.

  2. 2

    Clip in

    Wear it as usual. The felt keeps the oil off your skin and lets the scent rise around you.

  3. 3

    Refill

    One dose lasts about a day. A 5 ml bottle lasts three to four weeks of daily wear.

Our story

Our story

Summer evenings in Cyprus are long and warm. We wanted something lovely to wear in them.

Cliptique began with a simple idea: a hair clip that carries a scent the way a sachet does. Each top is printed from plant-based PLA, finished by hand and set with a pad of pure wool felt.

We make everything at home in batches of fifty, so colours change with the seasons and nothing sits in a warehouse. When the scent fades, you refill the felt and keep the clip.

Care and questions

Care and questions

How long does the scent last?

Three to five drops on the felt last about a day. A 5 ml bottle holds roughly 100 drops, enough for three to four weeks of daily wear.

Can I put the oil on my skin?

No. The blends are made for the wool felt only. Keep them away from your eyes and skin, and wash your hands after refilling. If you have sensitive skin or allergies, check the ingredients on the label first.

Are the clips suitable for children?

Children over three can wear them with an adult adding the oil. The clips contain small parts, so they are not for children under three.

How do I clean the felt or change the scent?

Let the felt air out for a day, then add the new blend. Wipe the clip with a soft, dry cloth and keep it out of water.

When will my order ship?

Our first batch ships in March 2027. Pre-orders are made in the order they arrive. Delivery in Cyprus costs €3.50 and is free over €35. We ship to the EU by tracked mail.
