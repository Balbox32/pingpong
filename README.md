## 🎈 Baaalloon! — starring Nil & Feta the goat

A mobile-first 2D sky-climber in [`baaalloon/index.html`](baaalloon/index.html). It's one self-contained file: every character, sound and song is drawn or synthesised live in the browser, with no image or audio assets. Open it on a phone (or any browser) and play.

**How to play**
- **Drag** anywhere (or ← → / A D) to steer Feta and Nil.
- **Tap** (or Space) to **BAAA-dash**. Feta headbutts straight up and smashes anything in the way. With no balloons left, the same button **flaps**.
- Eat tin cans, clovers, cheese stars and golden apples to build **combos** and fill Nil's **SASS** meter.
- When SASS is full, tap Nil's face (or press E) for **Nil's Unimpressed Stare**, which turns every enemy on screen to stone.
- Each hit pops a balloon. More balloons means a faster climb, but also a bigger target.

**Worlds:** The Pasture → Cloud Kingdom → Thunder Belt (telegraphed lightning) → Low Orbit (UFO tractor beams, satellites, meteors) → The Cheese Nebula, and then, beyond space, worlds that change how the whole game looks:
- **Sky Ocean** (1500 m): Nil and Feta wear snorkels. Jellyfish, puffing pufferfish, kelp, light rays, and the sound goes muffled underwater.
- **Real Life** (1900 m): every outline disappears for soft shading, shadows, bloom, a sunset with lens flares, and cinematic black bars.
- **8-Bit Zone** (2300 m): the screen pixelates and gets scanlines, with pixel invaders, pixel coins, a synthwave sun and grid, and chiptune music.
- **Platypus World** (2700 m): Nil, Feta and every enemy turn into platypuses, including secret agents in fedoras with jetpacks. Feta's bleat becomes a platypus chatter.

On the title screen you can warp straight to any world.

**Random mini-games:** every 30–50 seconds the climb pauses for a surprise mini-game, never the same one twice in a row. Win to get +250 points, +2 balloons and a sass boost.
- **Pump It Up!** Tap like crazy so Nil can pump up a giant balloon before time runs out. It slowly deflates, so don't stop. At 100% Feta floats away.
- **Snack Attack!** Drag Feta to catch 8 falling snacks in 8 seconds. Don't eat Nil's phone: that costs 2 snacks, and Nil screams.
- **Headbutt Showdown!** Feta vs. Big Gary (sunglasses). Tap when the swinging needle is in the yellow zone. Best 2 of 3, and the needle speeds up each round. In Platypus World it's Agent Gary.

When you fall, Nil gives a verdict on your run, from *“Nil is unimpressed.”* up to *“NIL SMILED.”*
