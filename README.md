# pingpong

## HEXVIPER

A cyberpunk snake × twin-stick shooter that runs in the browser. Open `hexviper/index.html` — no build step, no dependencies.

- **Feed** — kills drop shards; every shard adds a segment and every ten segments raise your score multiplier.
- **Shield** — your body blocks bullets (each one burns a segment); anything that rams it cuts the tail off.
- **Close the circuit** — steer your head into your own body to delete everything inside the loop.
- Eight enemy types, two alternating bosses every fifth sector, fourteen stackable augments, dash, overclock, procedural synth soundtrack.

Controls: `WASD` steer · mouse aim, hold to fire · `Space` dash · `E` overclock · `F` auto-fire · `P` pause · `M` mute. Touch (twin thumbs) and gamepad are supported.

## Ping pong

`ping_pong.py` is the original pygame ping-pong game.
